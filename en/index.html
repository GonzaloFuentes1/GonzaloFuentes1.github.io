Projects

Open-source code for LLMs

  • limpia

    Pretraining data curation for Latin American Spanish and Portuguese text: Gopher and FineWeb quality rules, MinHash deduplication, personal-data scrubbing and benchmark decontamination.

  • parallelism-from-scratch

    Data parallelism, ZeRO stages 1 and 2, and tensor and pipeline parallelism on a tiny Llama, each checked against a float64 single-process reference.

  • post-training-from-scratch

    SFT with packing, DPO, IPO, SimPO, GRPO and DAPO written from scratch, with tests that check the properties of each objective.

Personal projects

  • LLM Learning Route

    An open learning route, written in Spanish, for building LLMs from the fundamentals to the state of the art, with hands-on guides and code.

  • dreamer4

    Unofficial PyTorch re-implementation of Dreamer 4: tokenizer, dynamics and imagination RL on DMControl.

  • TetrisNNA-GA

    An AI that learns to play Tetris: a genetic algorithm evolves its evaluation function over 13 board features, with games running in parallel.

    Watch the demo on YouTube

  • BielsIA

    A soccer transfer recommender built on graph neural networks (GCN, GAT, Graphormer) that models how a player would fit into a new team.

  • light-anonymizer

    A desktop app that anonymizes PDFs and images without them leaving the computer, built for Chilean public officials who publish documents: it finds IDs, names, faces and signatures, and produces an audit report.
